# Brief: the bush slice in the training gate, and a chibi app icon

You are the art and animation specialist on Kotodama, an offline-first PWA (React + TypeScript + Vite) that teaches Japanese as a game. Claude owns the code and will review, test and merge your work.

## House style

Chibi and cute, the direction you set earlier: big heads, small bodies, round chunky shapes, flat saturated fills, a darker bottom shade band under every form, one soft top-left highlight, no thin outlines. Japanese-themed, original (not Duolingo's characters). It must read at 40–120 px, in light and dark themes.

## Task 1: slice the bushes open

In the training gate (`Gate`, `Bushes` and `SceneArt` in `src/foundations/moments/MomentsLayer.tsx`, styles in `src/foundations/moments/moments.css`), replace the bushes simply sliding apart with a sword slice that cuts the bush wall open:

1. A fast blade stroke (a bright streak with a soft trailing glow) sweeps across the bush wall on a diagonal, early in the gate (about 10–22% of `--gate-ms`).
2. The cut is not a ruler-straight line. It is a slightly jagged, organic line, like a real cut through leaves: small irregular zig-zags and nicks along a mostly diagonal path, with a few leaf scraps and cut sprigs flying off along it.
3. The bush wall splits along exactly that jagged line into two pieces (for example, matching jagged `clip-path: polygon(...)` edges on two copies of the bush layers). The pieces slide and tumble apart along the cut (the upper piece up and away, the lower piece down and away, with a little rotation, overshoot and settle), revealing the scene behind. The mid bush layer should follow the same cut, slightly later, for depth.
4. Call `haptic('thud')` (already imported in that file) on the frame the blade finishes its stroke.
5. Keep the rest of the gate as it is: the mist clearing, lanterns lighting, the avatar walking in, the `--gate-ms` and `--fade-ms` timeline, tap to skip, and the hand-over to the UI.

## Task 2: chibi app icon

The app icon is a flat red 言 hanko seal, generated by `seal()` in `tools/make-icons.ts` (which writes `public/icons/seal.svg` and the PNGs; Claude will run it). Redesign `seal()` in the chibi style: for example a cute round word-spirit (kotodama) character with a big head and tiny body, holding or wearing the 言 seal, on the same warm background, so it still reads as Kotodama at 32 px and as a home-screen icon. Keep the function signature and the `padding` behavior (the maskable icon uses bigger padding). This file renders to PNG, so use literal hex colors there, taken from `design/tokens.json`.
